# AI Opportunity Report: Lead Qualification Assistant for Cynbit

## 1. Executive Summary

This project explores how AI can help Cynbit review customer inquiries more easily. The Lead Qualification Assistant extracts important project details, identifies missing information, and assigns a priority level to help the team decide which inquiries to review first.

## 2. About Cynbit

Cynbit's public website describes services related to AI automation, custom software development, and cloud solutions. Its website also provides a contact page where potential customers can submit project inquiries.

Sources:

- https://www.cynbit.in/
- https://www.cynbit.in/services
- https://www.cynbit.in/contact

## 3. Problem and Opportunity

When a company receives project inquiries, its team needs to understand each customer's requirements, check whether important details are available, and decide what to do next.

Reviewing these inquiries manually may take time, especially when many messages need attention. An AI assistant could help by summarizing inquiries and identifying missing information.

This is a possible opportunity based on Cynbit's public website. I do not have access to Cynbit's internal processes or data, so the actual size of this problem has not been confirmed.

## 4. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a Lead Qualification Assistant that allows a team member to enter a customer inquiry and receive:

- A short summary and project category.
- The customer's main requirements.
- Budget and timeline, if mentioned.
- Missing information and suggested questions.
- A High, Medium, or Low priority based on fixed rules.
- Suggested next steps and a downloadable JSON result.

The team reviews the results before making decisions or contacting customers.

## 5. Expected Benefits

- **Save review time:** Summarize customer inquiries quickly.
- **Consistent review:** Apply the same scoring rules to each inquiry.
- **Identify missing details:** Highlight information needed before discussing a project.
- **Support decision-making:** Help the team organize inquiries by priority.

These are expected benefits and would need to be tested with real results before claiming measurable improvements.

## 6. Other Solutions Considered

1. **AI support assistant:** Answers questions using company documents, but requires suitable documentation.
2. **Document processing:** Extracts information from client documents, but needs sample documents and testing.
3. **Proposal generation:** Helps prepare proposals, but accurate cost and timeline estimates require reliable project data.
4. **Lead qualification:** Helps review project inquiries and identify missing information. This was selected because it is focused and can be demonstrated with a small prototype.

## 7. How to Measure Success

If Cynbit decides to test this idea, the team could measure:

- How accurately the app extracts project details.
- How often its priority matches human review.
- Whether it correctly identifies missing information.
- Whether it reduces the time spent reviewing inquiries.
- How often users need to correct the results.

These measures should be tested against an approved set of sample inquiries.

## 8. Risks and Solutions

- **Incorrect information:** Ask users to verify the extracted details.
- **Incorrect priority:** Keep the scoring rules visible and allow human review.
- **Missing budget or timeline:** Show these as not stated instead of guessing.
- **Customer privacy:** Use sample inquiries during the demo and avoid sharing confidential information without approval.
- **Unproven benefits:** Test the application before claiming time savings or business improvements.

## 9. Conclusion

The Lead Qualification Assistant is a practical idea to explore because it relates to Cynbit's publicly described services and project inquiry process.

The prototype demonstrates how AI and Python can work together to organize customer inquiries. Further testing and feedback from Cynbit would be needed to determine whether it provides real business value.
